Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 1-3 business days. Express delivery is available for an additional fee and arrives within 24 hours. Delivery times may vary based on your location and product availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money, credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard), and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address, and we'll send you a link to reset your password. The link will expire in 24 hours for security reasons.

What is your return policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original condition with tags attached. Some products like perishable goods and personalized items cannot be returned.

Do you deliver on weekends?

Yes, we offer weekend delivery for an additional fee. You can select weekend delivery during checkout. Standard deliveries are Monday to Friday.
