Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our VIP program

What payment methods do you accept for VIP subscriptions?

We accept Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money, and all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard). All payments are processed securely through our encrypted payment gateway.

Can I cancel my VIP subscription at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your VIP subscription at any time through your account settings. Your benefits will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, after which they will be discontinued.

Is there a free trial available for VIP packs?

We occasionally offer free trials for new members. Please check our promotions page or contact our support team to see if any trial offers are currently available.

How does the cashback system work?

Cashback is automatically calculated as a percentage of your order total (excluding delivery fees and taxes) and credited to your Delevva wallet within 24 hours of order completion. You can use these credits for future orders or withdraw them according to our cashback policy.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my VIP pack?

Yes, you can change your VIP pack at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference for the remainder of your billing cycle. When downgrading, the change will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
